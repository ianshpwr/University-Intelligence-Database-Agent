You pay fees one school term at a time (not the entire year at once). Your exact fees will be posted to your student account approximately a month before the start of each term. Incidental fees include items such as health and dental plans; your transit pass; recreation facilities; student clubs; support from career advisors and counsellors. Co-op students pay a fee of $836 per term to cover some costs of the co-op program. The number of times you pay the co-op fee varies by program. If you're in a co-op program, you don't pay tuition during your co-op work terms .
